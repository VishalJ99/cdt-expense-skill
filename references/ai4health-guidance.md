# AI4Health CDT: conference approval and expense-claim rules

Applies to the UKRI Centre for Doctoral Training in AI for Healthcare and the UKRI AI Centre for Doctoral Training in Digital Healthcare at Imperial College London (together, "AI4Health").

Sources, paraphrased (not verbatim):

- **[G]** *Conference and travel costs – Eligibility & Approval guidance*, AI4Health, **2025-26 edition** (last revised October 2025), sent to all cohorts in January 2026. Section names follow the PDF.
- **[E]** *AI4Health CDT Conf Cost estimates for approval* spreadsheet, 2025-26, in `assets/`.
- **[A, month year]** repeated written instructions from the AI4Health CDT administrator / programme manager to students when returning or correcting claims.
- **[P]** practice from accepted claims, not a written rule; follow it unless told otherwise.

**Check for a newer edition.** The guidance is reissued each academic year. If the CDT has sent a newer guidance PDF, or the administrator's latest email to you says something different, that wins over this file. Note the difference in your reply.

## Before you book: approval

1. Agree the conference with your supervisors first, then ask the CDT administrator for approval before booking anything. [G, Principles and responsibilities]
2. Start early: at least 3 months ahead for ICML, MICCAI and NeurIPS. [G, Principles and responsibilities]
3. Funding normally covers about one leading global AI conference a year. Your first needs no contribution; after that you need an accepted paper or poster (main conference or workshop). A relevant summer school may count; ask the CDT. If acceptance is still pending, any approval is conditional and lapses automatically if the paper isn't accepted. [G, Overview; Principles and responsibilities]
4. Request approval with the cost-estimate spreadsheet (`assets/AI4Health_conference_cost_estimate_template.xlsx`). Give the conference name, city, website, dates, travel dates, eligible nights and your contribution (say if you are first author, and link the paper). Include an FX rate if costs are in a foreign currency, a screenshot of hotel search results with two comparison hotels, and the hotel's refund policy (non-refundable bookings may only be reimbursed after the trip). [E]
5. An approved cost estimate is not a claim, and approval does not prove any amount was spent. [P]
6. Check visa needs yourself, and register every trip with Imperial's travel insurance. The insurance covers College business only, not holidays. [G, Principles and responsibilities; Travel Insurance]

## What is claimable

7. Eligible costs: conference fees, travel, accommodation and subsistence. All are subject to approval. [G, Overview]
8. **Cap: £3,000 per trip.** [G, Principles and responsibilities]
9. Eligible dates: travel out the day before the conference starts and home the day after it ends. Hotel nights are limited to the same window, so check out the next day. [E]
10. Use public transport wherever possible and safe, including to and from airports. Taxis are not normally covered; if you take one, justify it in the claim, and even then it may be refused. [G, Tips on how to keep the costs in check; E]
11. Seat pre-selection is not covered. [E]
12. Compare hotels and short lets rather than defaulting to the conference hotel. Avoid hostels for safety. Be prudent: costs are checked against UKRI and Imperial policy, and anything that doesn't comply is declined. [G, Tips on how to keep the costs in check; Principles and responsibilities; E]
13. Subsistence means meals bought on travel and conference days. Maximum rates apply, and dinners out every night are not acceptable. [E]
14. **No alcohol, including beer.** It is ineligible under UKRI rules, so pay for it separately where you can. If it is on a shared receipt, leave it out of the claimed amount. [G, Tips on how to keep the costs in check]
15. Tips are not listed in the guidance. In practice claims have left tips out, and kept unavoidable payment-service fees in with a note. Say what you included in the email. [P]

## How to claim

16. Pay first yourself, then claim through Imperial's expense procedure. [G, How to claim expenses]
17. Use **Imperial's Expense Claim Form (E1) for non-employees** in its **original Excel format**, never PDF. Fill it in yourself; don't regenerate or rebuild it, because a reconstructed sheet lost its lookups and had to be redone. [G, How to claim expenses; A, Jul 2025, Sep 2025, Jun 2026, Sep 2026]
18. Leave the form's structure alone. Don't add rows, and keep the analysis-code formulas. If a section is full, start a second form. [A, Nov 2025]
19. **Purpose of claim:** `<Your surname>- AI4Health CDT-Conference expenses`. [G, How to claim expenses]
20. Conference registration goes in *Subsistence / Hotels / Incidentals / Others*, with the type chosen from the *Expense Type* dropdown (`CONFERENCE FEES`). [G, How to claim expenses]
21. **Leave the cost centre and analysis codes to the administrator,** who adds the cost centre to every claim. Don't type codes in. [G, How to claim expenses; A, Nov 2025]
22. **Foreign currency:** enter the original amount in the *Foreign Currency* column and the GBP equivalent for the transaction date in *Sterling total*. Any online converter is accepted, but say which source and rates you used. [G, How to claim expenses]
23. **Receipts must be itemised,** showing date, item or unit costs and vendor. Payment confirmations alone and card statements are not accepted. [G, How to claim expenses]
24. **Translate** foreign-language receipts (e.g. Google Translate) and include the translation with the receipt. [G, How to claim expenses]
25. **Receipts as JPG**, one file per receipt, named starting with your surname. Adding the date and amount is the suggested detail, e.g. `Surname_20260926_Vendor_21.80EUR.jpg`. [G, How to claim expenses; A, May 2025, Jul 2025, Sep 2025, Aug 2026]
26. **One claim per email:** the Excel form plus one ZIP of exactly that form's receipts. You can submit as many forms as you need, each in its own email. [G, How to claim expenses; A, Nov 2025, Aug 2026]
27. **Filenames:** letters, digits and underscores only, with no dashes or special characters. [A, Oct 2026]

## After the trip

28. Email the CDT a short account of the conference: what you heard, what was useful, and anything that inspired your own research. It feeds UKRI reporting. [G, Action after returning from the conference]
29. Acknowledge UKRI funding in every output: papers, posters, slides and chapters. Cite the grant number in square brackets: **EP/S023283/1** if you started 2019–2023 (*UKRI CDT in AI for Healthcare*), **EP/Y030974/1** if you started in 2024 or later (*UKRI AI CDT in Digital Healthcare*). [G, Acknowledgment of your UKRI sponsorship]
